---
title: How to play
---

You are a samurai on the road, and every question in your quiz is an ambush. Answer well and you clear the stage. Answer badly too often and you fall.

## The run

The samurai runs on his own, at a steady pace that never speeds up. There is nothing to steer. The only button is pause, top right.

## An ambush

When ninjas spring out, the world slows down and a scroll unrolls with the question and its options. Every option has an arrow next to it.

- **Swipe toward an arrow** in the paper target at the foot of the scroll to pick that option. Only swipes on the target count, so you can scroll the question freely above it.
- **One answer:** the first swipe strikes.
- **Several answers, or an order:** swipe each one, then lift your finger and wait a moment to strike. For an order, swipe them in the right order. A swipe is final.
- **Yes or no:** swipe up for yes, down for no.

Take too long and the ninjas strike first. Novice has no time limit.

## The life bar

The life bar is not health, it is your margin: full means a perfect score is still possible, empty means the quiz's pass mark is out of reach. Every miss takes that question's share off the bar. When it runs out, you fall.

## Score

Every right answer is worth 100 points. Your run time is shown as well: with equal points, the faster run holds the high score. Only a run that passes sets a high score.

## After the run

The results show every mistake with your answer, the right one and why. Practise them in the **dojo**, where there is no timer, no life bar and no score.
